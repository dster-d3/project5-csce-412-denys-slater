AwShucks Oyster Bar:

When I was in my senior year of high school, I worked at an AwShucks Oyster Bar and Grill location in Frisco as well. A large portion of the restaurant’s demographic spoke only Spanish. Much of my contribution to the team was in taking orders for these people and communicating with the kitchen staff that also only spoke Spanish. For new hires, I helped teach those willing to learn the menu in spanish which greatly improved the company's workflow as it enabled more people to take orders greatly improving wait times.

Grace Bridge Resale:

In the past I have volunteered at Grace Bridge Resale in Frisco, Texas for a few weeks. At Grace Bridge Resale I hung new clothes up and organized the clothing racks, electronics and home decor into more visually appealing configurations. I also helped process donations and sort them into their respective categories. The work I did at Grace Bridge Resale helped me further develop a strong sense of duty unto others.
