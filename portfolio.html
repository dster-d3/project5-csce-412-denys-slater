Reddit Bot Overview:

I set out to create a bot that when given a link to a Reddit story page and some b-roll it would scrape the page for the story text and title, generate audio and subtitles and combine the audio and subtitles with a provided background video. After I was satisfied with my final product I uploaded Reddit story videos on TikTok as a proof of concept. Though they didn’t get many views, the entire process proved the capabilities that computer science could provide and secured my loyalty to the discipline.

Blackjack Overview:

During my freshman year at Texas A&M in their general engineering program I was introduced to python for the first time and slowly began to fall in love with coding. Towards the end of the semester, I worked on creating my own game and really enjoyed the process of getting better at learning new libraries and reading documentation. I enjoyed it so much that over winter break I continued to work on adding graphics as well as a current card count tracker
