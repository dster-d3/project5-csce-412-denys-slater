About Me:

Howdy! My name is Denys Slater and I’m an undergraduate computer science student at A&M and aspiring to work in quantitative finance. I’ve had severe issues with dyslexia in my youth but with the help of my parents and the fine teachers at Shelton I can proudly say that I haven’t needed any accommodations since middle school. I’m no stranger to adversity and love to conquer challenging and complex tasks. A lot of my motivation to pursue stem was born from the frustration of feeling incapable and release I felt when I finally reached a level of mastery over my dyslexia.

A large part of my confidence comes from my ability to teach or convey complex concepts to others. This began in my group tutoring for dyslexics where I learned to convey the same concepts differently depending on a person's needs. I love being able to work with people and reach a common understanding whatever that may be:

  • A solution to an issue
  • Resolving miscommunication and misunderstandings
  • Orienting towards a new goal or project
  • Communicating details about a project and its construction

Though I especially enjoy aiding others in clearing their confusions, I hope to be an asset to those around me in all manners of life so clearing confusion is not something I prioritize over being a good listener and student. In school projects, I’ve been able to help facilitate a positive, friendly, and efficient group environment. It’s been obvious to me from a young age that communication is the most important aspect of any relationship and I constantly employ and improve upon my communication skills. Furthermore, the confidence I have in my ability to convey complex ideas to others extends to my communication skills. You can only understand what somebody doesn't understand or needs from you by listening closely and you can only convey complex ideas by tailoring your explanation to the recipient.
